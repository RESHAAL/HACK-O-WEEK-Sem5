1. Objective
To study the basic intuition and applications of PCA and t-SNE and understand how they reduce and visualize high-dimensional data.

2. Principal Component Analysis (PCA)
Studied PCA as a dimensionality reduction technique that transforms multiple features into a smaller number of principal components while preserving the most important information in the data.

3. PCA Applications
Understood the use of PCA for reducing feature dimensions, simplifying datasets, removing redundant information, and improving data visualization.

4. t-SNE
Studied t-SNE as a dimensionality reduction technique mainly used for visualizing high-dimensional data in 2D or 3D by preserving relationships between similar data points.

5. t-SNE Applications
Understood the application of t-SNE for data visualization, identifying clusters, exploring patterns, and analyzing similarities between data points.

6. PCA vs t-SNE
Compared PCA and t-SNE based on their purpose and behavior. PCA focuses on preserving important variance through linear transformation, while t-SNE focuses mainly on representing local similarities for visualization.

7. Tools Used
Python
NumPy
Scikit-learn
Matplotlib
VS Code

8. Outcome
Gained a basic understanding of dimensionality reduction, PCA, and t-SNE, along with their applications in reducing and visualizing high-dimensional datasets.
