Implemented LightGBM for efficient gradient boosting and studied its application in classification tasks.

Studied the bias-variance trade-off and understood the relationship between model complexity, underfitting, overfitting, and generalization.

Compared training and testing performance to understand overfitting and underfitting and studied methods to prevent them.
